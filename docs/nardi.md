# ნარდი · Classical backgammon

Open **თამაშები → ნარდი**. Choose a 1-, 3-, 5-, or 7-point match, create a two-seat table, and invite an online account or join an open room. Invitations use the dashboard and notification bell, expire after 45 seconds, and enforce a one-minute cooldown after a decline. Hosts can remove waiting players. Free bot play is also available to guests.

The game follows the international section of the [Georgian federation rules](https://geonardi.com/rules/classical), with the [Rogor beginner guide](https://www.rogor.ge/article/548-rogor-vitamashot-nardi-damcqebtatvis/) for basic movement and the [USBGF guide](https://usbgf.org/backgammon-basics-how-to-play/) for the forced-dice and bearing-off details. It uses a standard 24-point setup and 15 checkers per side. The server rolls both opening dice and breaks ties; subsequent rolls use independent uniform dice. It validates the maximum playable dice sequence, higher-die priority, four moves on doubles, hits, bar priority, legal bearing off, and 1/2/3-point Oini/Mars/Cox. The international doubling cube changes hand points only. Crawford disables it for one hand, and a cube already sufficient to win the match cannot be offered again. The Georgian rulebook is in the game menu.

Tap a highlighted checker, then a destination; tap a die to choose its length when needed. Both players see the board and the rolled dice. Undo restores the last checker move within the unconfirmed turn, with the same dice. Finish is available only when no further legal move remains. A fully blocked roll passes automatically. The board is oriented to each player's home side. Its wood, pieces, pip dice and point highlights are drawn locally; phone landscape keeps the full board width and controls below it.

Both human players confirm the **30-site-coin** reservation before starting. The winner receives 58 of the 60 reserved coins, a net gain of 28; the loser loses 30 and 2 are removed. The cube and match length do not change that stake. Settlement is server-controlled and idempotent. Account records persist unfinished reservations so a shutdown or restart refunds them once. Only human matches affect the separate weekly Nardi ranking, which resets Monday at 00:00 Tbilisi time. Bot matches do not stake coins or change rankings.

The full turn has 60 seconds, with no clock reset for a checker move or undo. Cube answers have 30 seconds. A timeout loses the hand; two consecutive timeouts lose the match. Disconnects pause the clock for a 40-second rejoin window. Opening the Nardi tile or the current room URL resumes the same active seat. Replacement tabs invalidate the old socket. Leaving explicitly forfeits the match. A process restart refunds unfinished stakes and clears the in-memory boards.

Validation: **153 tests** pass with `node --test tests/*.test.js`, including 17 Nardi tests and 40 seeded full bot matches that conserve all 30 checkers. `node scripts/check-nardi.cjs` uses the real page and server module with local accounts and controlled positions, checks dashboard invitations, stakes, move/undo/finish, hitting/bar entry, cube decisions, chat, a bearing-off payout, bot resumption, and isolation from the saved chat theme. It checks desktop and five phone sizes from 320×568 portrait to 568×320 landscape. Playwright, Express, and Socket.IO must be available; `NODE_PATH` can point to a separate QA dependency directory, `CITY_QA_CHROME` selects an installed browser, and `NARDI_QA_OUTPUT` selects screenshot output. No new runtime dependencies or external AI services are used.
