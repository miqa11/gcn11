# Bar drinks and arcade readiness

At the garden bartender, Radio Bar doorway or casino bar, the nearby **ბარი** button opens a compact menu. Beer costs 50 site coins, wine 100 and a cocktail 150. Registered accounts can pay for themselves or offer a drink to another player at the same bar. Recipients choose accept/decline. Acceptance rechecks proximity, presence and the buyer's balance, then charges the buyer once. Declined/expired/disconnected offers cost nothing. An offer expires after 45 seconds; it never forces intoxication on another player. Prices/effects are server-owned. Recent receipt IDs persist with account data to reject duplicate purchases; balances use the existing shared coin helpers.

Each drink adds 30 seconds of temporary intoxication, capped at 90 seconds and strength 3. The effect sways only the world canvas, leaving HUD buttons steady; reduced-motion settings suppress the animation. It is suppressed during shared games, rides, driving and locked activities. Free water ends the effect immediately. Offers/effects are session-only and clear on disconnect; no real money or provider service is involved.

Selecting a solo arcade cabinet now opens a ready screen with **დაწყება**. The game simulation, controls and 60-second timer remain idle until that explicit click. The selected cabinet replaces the scrollable list in a fitted play view, with a visible back button and controls. Landscape uses side controls and hides the cabinet-list tabs until returning. Competitive arcade matches retain their server-synchronized start/deadline. Records callbacks preserve the current run, and refreshing the versus tab replaces its list instead of appending duplicates.

Validation uses server tests for balances, forged prices, purchase replay, gift acceptance/decline/expiry/distance, occupied states and water. Local three-client browser QA checks self/gift payment and effect clearing, all four solo cabinets remaining idle until Start, no scrolling in 320/390 px portrait and 667/844 px landscape, desktop start, and the shared zombie boss appearing and dying from actual shots. These are local fixtures; production network/device balance still needs live play.

Repeat the browser check with `node scripts/check-city-bar-arcade.cjs` after the optional Playwright setup in [city-mobile-layout.md](city-mobile-layout.md). It starts an ephemeral local fixture and writes screenshots to the OS temporary directory (or `CITY_QA_OUTPUT`). Set `CITY_QA_CHROME` to use an installed Chrome/Chromium. Boss spawning in this check is accelerated by clearing regular enemies on the server; the automated server tests separately shoot through all ten regular waves and bosses.
