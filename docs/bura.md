# Bura

Open **ბურა** in the games dashboard. Create a two-seat table, invite online registered players, or join an open table. Invitations also appear on the dashboard and in existing game notifications. Both seated players must press **მზად ვარ · 30 მონეტა** before a paid match starts. The host can remove a waiting player. A declined invitation has a 60-second cooldown; invitations expire after 45 seconds. Guests can play the free bot.

The supplied Georgian rulebook is available in the game: 20 cards, three-card hands, ace/ten/king/queen/jack order, same-suit leads, explicit cut or hidden discard, concealed captured points, 31-point Var declarations, automatic Bura and double-value royal Bura, alternating bidding through Shashi, Crawford, out-of-turn Malutka, and matches to 11. Successful cut matching is independent of selection order. The exposed trump stays at the bottom of the deck. Completed tricks clear after two seconds, then the taker can declare Var or continue. On continue, refills alternate one card at a time, starting with the taker, so the final hands remain equal and every lead can receive an equal-sized response. The responder can bid before playing their cards and resumes the same trick after acceptance. Royal Bura has priority over ordinary Bura. Crawford applies once per match; tied rounds alternate the next starter. These details are stated in the Georgian rulebook.

An action has 30 seconds. A timeout loses the round (a bid response concedes at the previous price); a second consecutive timeout loses the match. A successful action resets that player's timeout streak. A disconnected player has 40 seconds to rejoin; the action clock pauses and resumes with the remaining time. A refresh or replacement tab rejoins the same seat; the old tab cannot act or receive private state. Leaving a running match forfeits it. Results remain available in the round-history dialog.

Paid matches reserve 30 existing site coins from each player through the shared wallet helpers. The winner receives 58 from the 60 reserved coins: their original 30 plus 28 profit. The loser receives none, for a net loss of 30. Exactly 2 coins are removed. Bidding changes match points only. Settlement is server-side and once-only. The escrow fields persist with user records. Graceful shutdown refunds unfinished stakes; startup also recovers any persisted unfinished reservation once. A process restart ends the in-memory match rather than resuming it. No points or coins are settled from a client result claim.

Only paid matches affect the weekly Bura leaderboard. Wins, losses and net Bura coins persist on each account. The week starts Monday at 00:00 in Tbilisi; previous-week entries disappear immediately on reads. Bot games never stake coins or update this leaderboard. The local bot receives only `viewForSeat`, including its own hand and the same masked history a human receives. It has no access to the deck, the opponent's hand, actual concealed points, or the opponent's hidden discarded faces.

`bura-engine.js` supplies shared pure helpers; `bura-game.js` owns the rules and redacted views; `server-bura.js` owns authenticated seats, invitations, clocks, escrow, reconnects and ranking; `bura-bot.js` chooses from a redacted view. The client uses the shared SVG deck and felt artwork with a dedicated control column in phone landscape. All new player-facing copy is Georgian. No new runtime dependency, AI key or external service is needed.

Validation: **136 tests**, including 14 Bura tests, pass with `node --test tests/*.test.js`. Two hundred seeded bot-versus-bot matches finish without illegal moves, deadlocks or card/point loss. Tests cover hidden information, special hands, bidding, ties, timeouts, repeat settlement, refunds, rankings, invitation cooldowns, malformed packets, replacement tabs and host permissions.

Optional browser QA: `node scripts/check-bura.cjs` uses the production Bura module and page scripts with local test accounts and controlled hands. It covers dashboard invitation acceptance, both readiness confirmations, real action payloads, hidden discards, counteroffers, chat, Var results, settlement, ranking, bot reconnects and isolation from the saved chat theme. Screenshots and bounds checks cover desktop plus 844×390, 667×375, 568×320, 390×844 and 320×568. Playwright, Express and Socket.IO must be available; `NODE_PATH` can point to a separate dependency directory, `CITY_QA_CHROME` selects an installed Chrome, and `BURA_QA_OUTPUT` selects screenshot output. No production accounts or coins are used by this check.
